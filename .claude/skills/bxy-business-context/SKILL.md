# BxY Business Context

Source of truth: `docs/01-BUSINESS-MODEL.md`. Read it before producing commercial output.

## Hard facts (never change without the partners' approval)

- Entry product **BxY Commerce Core**: **$350.000 CLP + IVA (pago único)**.
- Includes lifetime custom storefront + dedicated backend (API + unified Admin + D1 + R2).
- No forced USD monthly fees. **0% commission per sale.**
- Founding case: **TripSeeds** (Blueprint 01).
- Partners: **Bolder (Iván)** — art direction, identity, UI/UX, packaging, labels, motion.
  **Yey** — Edge/Cloudflare, e-commerce engine, APIs, data science, BI, AI.

## Anti-Shopify argument (cannabis ecosystem / growshops / author brands)

1. No yearly USD drain (Shopify + patch apps + extra 2% on Webpay/MercadoPago/Flow).
2. Sovereignty: no risk of foreign-policy account closures for cannabis (seeds, terpenes, paraphernalia).
3. Edge speed on Cloudflare, no plugin bloat on mobile checkout.

## Upsell modules

Logistics (Starken, BlueExpress, Chilexpress) · Data Science & BI · Motion Graphics & AI content ·
Design & Packaging. Module prices are not defined: never invent them, say "a cotizar".

## Copy rules

- Customer-facing copy in Spanish (es-CL), direct and technical.
- Always write prices as net + IVA: `$350.000 + IVA`.
- Aesthetic: industrial/technical brutalist, monochrome, mono type, `1px solid` borders, no emojis.
- Do not make legal claims about cannabis legality; argue sovereignty and platform risk only.
